Delete Your Account

Last updated: July 2026

This page explains how to permanently delete your Calman account and the data stored with it. Calman is a calorie and body-composition tracker published by Haidar Al-Aubiydy. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete your account from inside the app

This is the fastest route and takes effect immediately.

  1. Open the Calman app and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm when asked.

Your account and data are erased straight away and you are signed out. There is no waiting period and no grace period — once confirmed, the data cannot be recovered.

Delete your account by email

If you can no longer sign in or cannot reach the app, email iamhaidar@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Calman account”, sent from the email address on the account. We will verify the request and delete the account within 30 days, usually within 72 hours, and email you to confirm once it is done.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently erases all of the following:

  • Your login and account record, including your email address
  • Your profile — name, date of birth, sex, height, goal weight, activity level, and your calorie and macro targets
  • Every logged meal, including the individual food items and their nutrition estimates
  • All meal photos you have taken or uploaded
  • All progress and body photos
  • All weigh-ins, including weight and body-fat readings
  • Saved meals in My Meals
  • Streaks, achievements, and badges
  • Your saved food preferences

This also covers any temporary guest session created before you signed up, along with the demo scans taken during it.

What is kept, and for how long

Nothing that identifies you remains in the live database. Your records are hard-deleted rather than flagged as inactive, and your stored photos are removed from storage at the same time.

Two narrow exceptions apply:

  • Encrypted backups. Our hosting provider keeps rolling encrypted database backups for operational recovery. A copy of your data may persist in those backups for up to 30 days after deletion, after which it is overwritten and unrecoverable. Backups are never used to restore an individual deleted account.
  • Purchase records. If you bought a subscription, the transaction record is held by Apple or Google as the payment processor, not by Calman, and is retained by them for tax and accounting purposes. To request deletion of that record, contact Apple or Google directly.

Calman does not sell your data and does not use it for advertising, before or after deletion.

Deleting some data without closing your account

You do not have to delete your whole account to remove individual entries. Inside the app you can delete any single logged meal, any individual weigh-in, and any progress photo, while keeping your account and the rest of your history intact.
