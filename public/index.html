Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

AI photo scan

Point your camera at a plate. Calman identifies the foods, estimates portions, and returns calories + macros with a confidence band — and asks a quick clarifying question when it matters, instead of guessing.

Result → log a full meal in seconds, with numbers you can trust.

Smart-scale body composition

Connect a Bluetooth scale and Calman logs your weight, body fat %, muscle, water, BMR and BMI — then charts the trend so you see real change, not daily noise.

Result → measure the change that matters, not just a single number.

Adaptive calorie & macro targets

Your daily target isn't a static guess. It learns from your logged intake and weight trend and suggests an update when your body adapts — manual or adaptive, your choice.

Result → a target that stays accurate as you progress.

Meal log & history

Every meal in a clean Day / Week / Month view with calorie bars, plus a full searchable history you can open and edit anytime.

Result → see exactly where your calories go, any day.

Personalized meal plans Coming soon

Turn your target into a real day of meals tailored to your goal, body and diet style — with one-tap logging and easy swaps that keep you on target.

Result → stop wondering what to eat to hit your numbers.
