Common questions

How accurate are the photo estimates? Every scan shows an honest confidence range, and Calman asks a clarifying question when an answer would meaningfully change the number. Tap any meal to correct items — it re-estimates instantly.

Why did my calorie target change? Your target adapts from your real logged intake and weight trend. You can set it manually any time by tapping the big number on Home.

How do I connect my smart scale? Log weight → Connect scale, with Bluetooth on. Calman reads weight and body composition directly.

How do I manage my subscription? Subscriptions are billed by the App Store — manage or cancel in your device's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Delete your account

In the app: Settings → Delete account. This permanently erases your account and all data — meals, weigh-ins, photos, and your plan. Prefer email? Write to iamhaidar@gmail.com from your account email and we'll complete it within 24 hours.
